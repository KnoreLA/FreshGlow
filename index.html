Visual Freshness Indicators

A simple, color-coded LED light proven to directly influence fridge and food waste habits.

GREEN STATUS: Safe & Fresh

Food spoilage is minimal. Safe to store. No immediate usage rush needed.

YELLOW STATUS: Eat Soon

Early gas fluctuations detected. Plan to eat within a few days.

RED STATUS: Choose Immediately

High gas degradation detected. Use it or lose it. Eat at your own risk.
